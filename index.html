Questions

How do you verify creators?

We check each creator's identity, confirm they own the channel they list, and review their portfolio before approving them.

When do creators get paid?

Payment sits in escrow after you book. It is released when you approve the finished content.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Every booking includes a revision round, and you can chat with the creator to explain what to change.

Who owns the content?

You get usage rights stated in the contract for each booking, including whether you can use it in paid ads.
